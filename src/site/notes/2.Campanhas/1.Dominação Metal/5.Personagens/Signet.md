# Signet, A Bruxa Tatuada
> Inspirada na Signet de DND

## Raça
Signet é uma meio-naia meio-elfa que fugiu de [[Shadowfell\|Shadowfell]] quando tinha 112 anos de idade, ela acabou indo parar no [[1.Phantyr/1.Phantyr/3.Localizações/Plano da Terra\|Plano da Terra]] sem querer e acabou permanecendo lá até os dias atuais

## Classe
Uma bruxa que luta com magia de sangue, necessitando de sacrificios para ativar suas tatuagens, que saem do seu corpo e atacam.

## Aparencia
Tsarina Signet stands about 5'6" tall and is extremely thin, with long pointed elf ears, short horns on her forehead, sharpened fangs, and faintly luminescent skin. She wears only a thong. From her toes to her face, she is covered in thick-lined, old-school tattoos of Asian- and nautical-themed monsters that move and swirl across her body. The tattoos can turn to stare at whoever she faces. When the tattoos leave her body, her skin shows as white with a faint blue shimmer, as if covered in very fine scales. Her eyes glow when she casts, and her hands glow yellow before she attacks.

Known tattoos include a three-headed ogre wielding a curved sword, dragons (among them a black sea dragon), Japanese oni, octopuses, and sharks. A hammerhead shark, a pair of moray-like eels with a lightning attack, a giant sea serpent, a floating head, and a small fish on her shoulder blade also appear.
